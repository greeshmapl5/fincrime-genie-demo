# Investigation procedure and boundaries

- **Domain:** Financial Crime
- **Synonyms:** case procedure, escalation steps, investigation steps
- **Description:** The steps an investigator follows, and what the assistant may and may not do.
- **Related assets and sources:** `fincrime.gold.alerts`, `fincrime.gold.structuring_candidates`, `fincrime.gold.case_notes`

## Definition

The investigation procedure is the ordered set of review steps applied to every alert. The investigator decides the outcome. The assistant provides analysis only.

## Business use

Steps
1. Review the alert and the customer profile.
2. Check whether the activity meets a documented typology: structuring, rapid pass-through or mule account.
3. Compare activity with expected monthly volume, using inflows only.
4. Review the counterparties that received funds.
5. Record the decision and the rationale: escalate, or close as a false positive with a reason.

Escalation
- A case with a typology match and total inflows above 90,000 SEK in 14 days is escalated to a senior investigator within 2 working days.

Assistant boundaries
- Provide analysis only. The investigator decides.
- Use neutral language, such as "consistent with" a typology.
- Do not state or imply that a customer is guilty, and do not describe purpose or intent.
- Do not suggest filing a report.
- Phrase next steps as neutral data checks.
