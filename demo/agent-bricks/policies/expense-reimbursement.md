# Expense Reimbursement Policy

> Fictional policy for the Databricks Summit demo. This document is not real company guidance.

## Eligible expenses

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business travel expenses that were approved by their manager before the trip. Receipts are required for every expense.

## Submission deadline

Submit an expense reimbursement request within 30 calendar days after the expense was incurred. Include the receipt and the manager's approval.

## Review and payment

Finance reviews complete requests. Approved reimbursements are paid through the next regular expense-payment cycle.

## Late requests

Requests submitted more than 30 calendar days after an expense require Finance review. Approval is not guaranteed. The policy does not specify an exception process or an appeal process.

## Policy scope

This document covers expense reimbursement only. It does not provide guidance about personal safety, travel emergencies, immigration documents, or other employee policies.
