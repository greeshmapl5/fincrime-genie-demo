Fictional investigation procedure (demo only, not regulatory guidance)

1. Review the alert and the customer profile.
2. Check whether the activity meets a documented typology (structuring, rapid pass-through, mule account).
3. Compare activity with expected monthly volume using inflows only.
4. Review counterparties that received funds.
5. Record the decision and rationale: escalate, or close as a false positive with a reason.

Escalation: a case with a typology match and total inflows above 90,000 SEK in 14 days is escalated to a senior investigator within 2 working days.

Boundaries: the assistant provides analysis only. The investigator decides. Use neutral language such as "consistent with". Do not suggest filing a report.
